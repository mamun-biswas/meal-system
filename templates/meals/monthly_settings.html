📐 Cost Calculation Formula
Step 1 — Meal Rate
Meal Rate =
Total Expense ÷
Total Effective Meals
Step 2 — Effective Meals
Eff. Meals =
Raw Meals × Count Weight
× Day Multiplier
Step 3 — Member Cost
Cost =
(Eff. Meals × Meal Rate)
+ Cooking Cost
Step 4 — Balance
Balance =
Deposit − Cost
+ = gets money back
− = owes money
